# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

**Our standard.** Be respectful and constructive. Welcome newcomers, assume good intent, give specific and kind feedback, and accept it graciously. Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, and publishing others' private information are not acceptable in issues, pull requests, discussions, or any project space.

**Enforcement.** Maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and may restrict participation of anyone who violates this standard. Report concerns privately through GitHub's "Report content" feature or by contacting a maintainer through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled confidentially.

By participating, you agree to follow this code of conduct.
